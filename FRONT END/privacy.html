Privacy Policy

Learn how NKP collects, uses and protects information provided through the Business Health Assessment platform.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

NKP (Namma KanakkuPillai) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using the Business Health Assessment platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and the steps taken to protect that information.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the platform, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Business name and business details.
  • Business assessment responses.
  • Assessment results and progress information.

3. Account Information

Account information is used to create and maintain your NKP account and to provide access to the platform's features.

Passwords should be kept confidential. You are responsible for protecting the credentials used to access your account.

4. Business Information

Information you provide about your business may be used to generate business health assessments, reports and recommendations.

Please avoid entering highly sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the assessment.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through the platform may be used to:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Provide business health assessments.
  • Generate assessment results and reports.
  • Provide business improvement recommendations.
  • Support AI-powered insights where enabled.
  • Maintain and improve the platform.
  • Help maintain platform security and reliability.

6. AI-Powered Features

Certain NKP features may use artificial intelligence to analyze assessment information and provide recommendations.

AI-generated recommendations are intended to provide informational guidance. They should be reviewed carefully before being used to make important business decisions.

7. Password Reset and Verification

If you use the password recovery feature, NKP may process your registered email address and a temporary verification code to confirm account ownership and allow you to create a new password.

Verification codes should not be shared with other people.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The platform may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to support features including login preferences and session-related information.

Your browser may provide controls for managing or clearing locally stored information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or technical systems where necessary to operate platform functionality, subject to applicable requirements.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform's services, maintain records, meet operational requirements or comply with applicable legal obligations.

12. Your Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when using the platform.
  • Keep your login credentials secure.
  • Do not share verification codes with others.
  • Review information before submitting an assessment.

13. Third-Party Services

Some platform functionality may depend on third-party technology or service providers. Their processing of information may be governed by their own policies and terms.

14. Children's Privacy

The platform is intended for business users and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page together with the updated revision date.

16. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information through the NKP platform, please contact the NKP platform administrator.
